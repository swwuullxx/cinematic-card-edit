# B-roll shot list

The B-roll should literally show the line being spoken. Keep clips moody, low-key and cinematic, with a shallow depth of field if possible.

| Line theme | Shot ideas | Grade |
|---|---|---|
| Work / grind / "put your head down" | Person at a laptop in a dark room, typing close-up, desk at night, whiteboard | Warm tungsten, dark |
| Ignore the noise / focus | Side profile staring off, headphones on, walking alone, closing a door | Teal / cold, low-key |
| Struggle / discipline | Snow, rain, running in the cold, a gym at 5am, sweat close-up | Desaturated blue-grey |
| Self-belief / "you can" | Head-down close-up then looking up, mirror shot, deep breath | Neutral, soft |
| Success / "show the world" | Suit in a luxury interior, car interior at night, city skyline, private-jet or yacht stock | Dark luxury, red/amber practicals |
| Speaker (A-roll) | You at a mic, dark background with **purple/blue + red rim light** | Punchy, saturated rim |

**Sourcing:** shoot your own, or use licensed stock (Pexels, Pixabay, Artgrid, Storyblocks). Only use film/TV clips if you have the rights.

**Speaker setup used in the style:** dark backdrop, purple/blue LED wash behind, red/orange rim light on one side, a podcast mic on a boom arm in frame, black t-shirt.
